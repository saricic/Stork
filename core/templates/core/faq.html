Driver FAQs

Answers to the most common questions from our drivers. Details may vary by city.

Can I drive with Stork in my city?

Stork is expanding, but we’re not available everywhere. Check supported cities or contact our team to see if Stork operates in your area.

How do I register to drive with Stork?

Fill in your details, upload required documents and submit your application. After approval and onboarding, you can start driving.

Do I need a car to drive with Stork?

Yes. If you don’t have a car, you can join partner fleets or use rental options depending on your city.

Do I need a smartphone to drive with Stork?

Yes — ride requests and navigation are handled in the driver app. A modern phone is recommended.

Is driving with Stork a good side hustle?

Yes. You choose when to go online. There are no minimum hours — drive evenings, weekends, or whenever you want.

How do I get paid for driving with Stork?

Payouts are processed weekly. You can track earnings and payout history in the app (UI placeholder).
